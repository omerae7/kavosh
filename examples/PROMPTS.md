# Kavosh Prompt Examples

These examples demonstrate intent and configuration. Users may specify the research depth and evidence rigor, or let Kavosh choose them.

## High-value product comparison

```text
Use Kavosh to compare Product A and Product B for professional field use.

Identify the exact models, current market, test conditions, total cost, reliability evidence, official specifications, independent testing, and recurring real-world problems. Do not declare an absolute winner if the result depends on user priorities.
```

## Primary-source tracing

```text
Use Kavosh to trace this claim to its earliest accessible primary source. Distinguish the original finding from later summaries, identify changes in wording or scope, and determine whether independent evidence confirms it.
```

## Scientific or health claim

```text
Use Kavosh with Sensitive evidence rigor to assess this claim. Match the study design to the question, check systematic reviews and current guidance, report effect size and uncertainty when available, distinguish statistical significance from practical importance, and avoid personalized medical advice.
```

## Current law or regulation

```text
Use Kavosh to determine the current rule in the specified jurisdiction. Start from the authoritative legal text, check effective dates, amendments, exceptions, and later guidance, and state the last verified date.
```

## Commercial claim audit

```text
Use Kavosh to audit this vendor claim. Separate what the vendor states from what independent evidence demonstrates. Check methodology, version, market, test conditions, conflicts of interest, and unresolved limitations.
```

## Real-world reliability

```text
Use Kavosh to investigate recurring real-world problems with this product. Separate anecdotes from supported patterns, check model and version alignment, look for technical evidence and official responses, and do not turn complaint counts into failure rates without a valid denominator.
```

## Causal claim

```text
Use Kavosh to assess whether X causes Y. Examine temporal order, confounding, reverse causality, selection and measurement bias, plausible mechanisms, alternative explanations, study design strength, and independent replication. Calibrate the conclusion to the evidence.
```

## Scenario and sensitivity analysis

```text
Use Kavosh to analyze this decision under the supplied scenarios. Use only user-provided or verified numeric inputs, identify the variables that change the result, calculate break-even points where possible, and separate robust findings from fragile ones.
```

## Contradictory sources

```text
Use Kavosh to explain why these credible sources disagree. Check version, date, jurisdiction, population, definitions, measurement methods, test conditions, corrections, independence, and conflicts of interest. Preserve any material conflict that cannot be resolved.
```
